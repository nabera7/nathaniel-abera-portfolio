Cryptography

Hands-on work with encryption, hashing, and public key infrastructure. In the lab I generated RSA and AES-256 keys and moved encrypted files between virtual machines, where the real lesson was key distribution and verifying who a public key truly belongs to. I then applied those concepts to a municipal scenario, analyzing authentication, integrity, and non-repudiation across payments, email, and stored evidence while identifying the vulnerabilities and stronger protocols that close each gap. Includes 13 lab screenshots.
